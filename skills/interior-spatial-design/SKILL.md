# Interior Spatial Design

## Objetivo

Organizar ambientes internos para uso, circulação, conforto e identidade visual, com furniture layouts dimensionais e maquete virtual coerente.

## Quando usar

- decoração/interiores;
- reforma de apartamento/casa/escritório;
- furniture layout;
- mood/material direction;
- 2D + 3D interior planner;
- virtual renovation;
- space planning.

## Workflow

1. **Existing shell**
   - room geometry;
   - openings;
   - fixed services;
   - structural constraints;
   - ceiling heights.

2. **Use scenarios**
   - users;
   - activities;
   - storage;
   - privacy;
   - seating/work/sleep needs.

3. **Zoning**
   - public/private/service;
   - activity clusters;
   - daylight/views;
   - circulation routes.

4. **Furniture layout**
   - real footprints;
   - circulation clearances;
   - door/cabinet swings;
   - focal point;
   - power/data/plumbing constraints when known.

5. **Lighting**
   - ambient/task/accent;
   - daylight;
   - glare;
   - fixture zones;
   - electrical validation remains separate.

6. **Materials**
   - floor/wall/ceiling;
   - durability;
   - maintenance;
   - reflectance/color;
   - moisture/fire/slip constraints flagged when relevant.

7. **Alternatives**
   - few distinct schemes;
   - compare storage, circulation, cost, light, flexibility and intervention level.

8. **3D mockup**
   - plan/3D parity;
   - consistent dimensions;
   - materials/camera for communication;
   - walkthrough/VR only as visualization.

9. **Renovation layers**
   - retain;
   - demolish;
   - construct;
   - furniture;
   - finish.

## Regras

- render cannot prove clearance, structure, electrical or plumbing feasibility;
- furniture scale must be real when testing circulation;
- decoration choices and technical specification are different deliverables;
- accessibility/code requirements need local grounding.

## Integração

`architectural-design-engineering`, `floor-plan-technical-drawing`, `building-performance-analysis`, `concept-to-3d-asset`.

## Provenance

Consolidada de Architect3D, Blueprint.js, Archilyse e SweetHome3D-style workflows, com 2D/3D parity e furniture-based space planning.
